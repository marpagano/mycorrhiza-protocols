## Notes recorded in the source

Use autoclaved glass jars with lids.

1. Take one living colonized root and cut it into 1 cm segments.
2. Wash.
3. Treat with 95% ethanol for 2–3 minutes.
4. Rinse with distilled water.
5. Treat with 2% sodium hypochlorite for 1–2 minutes.
6. Rinse three times with distilled water. The source includes “3 min” with an incomplete parenthesis; whether this applies to each rinse or the total is unclear.
7. Treat with streptomycin for 5–20 minutes. **Concentration missing.**
8. Rinse.
9. Place on a plate containing solid medium. **Medium and subsequent conditions missing.**
