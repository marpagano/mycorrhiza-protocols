## Culture plan recorded in the source

Soil samples **[amount missing]** collected in restoration areas will be used as inoculum for greenhouse trap cultures. Soil will be mixed with vermiculite/sand at a 2:1 ratio and placed in **[container description incomplete]**.

*Brachiaria decumbens* Stapf will be used because it is perennial, hardy and easy to maintain. A legume (*Medicago sativa* L.), *Plantago major* L. and *Cucumis sativus* L. will also be used. These species have been employed to multiply various AMF species in collections (Smith & Read, 1997).

The experiment will have four replicates per soil, with three plant species grown together. Plants will be irrigated with distilled water and fertilized every two weeks with 25 mL of Hoagland nutrient solution (Sylvia & Hubbell, 1986) containing a lower P concentration. All accessions will be assessed after multiplication.

Monospecific cultures will also be prepared (Jansa et al., 2002), using *Allium porrum* L. as the host plant. Spores from these cultures may be used to characterize potentially new species or for other studies.
