## Items requiring confirmation

The initial soil amount is missing. The source gives 50% or 60% sucrose without specifying the concentration basis. Centrifuge speeds are in RPM; rotor type and radius are missing. Do not convert these values to relative centrifugal force without that information. Confirm whether the sieve values refer to mesh openings; the source calls them diameters.

## Procedure recorded in the source

1. Starting with **[soil amount missing]**, wash the soil with tap water in a large beaker.
2. Pass it through sieves of approximately 100 and 45 µm (described as “diameter” in the source).
3. Repeat three times.
4. Transfer to centrifuge tubes and centrifuge at 3,000 RPM for 3 minutes.
5. Discard the supernatant. The source states that the tube wall is cleaned with a finger or paper; this handling instruction requires technical review.
6. Retain the pellet, add 50% or 60% sucrose, and mix.
7. Centrifuge at 2,000 RPM for 2 minutes.
8. Pass the supernatant through the smaller sieve and rinse gently with tap water.
9. Rinse with distilled water and transfer to a Petri dish.
10. Examine under a stereomicroscope.
