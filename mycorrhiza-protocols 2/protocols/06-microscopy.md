# Microscopy and slide mounting

[Back to index](../README.md)

**Status:** organized documentation pending technical review.

## Items requiring confirmation

Confirm preparation and coating requirements with the microscopy facility. The source gives an approximate time per sample without equipment context or validation.

## Scanning electron microscopy (SEM)

1. Sieve samples with distilled water, collect spores, and mount them on a small, thoroughly cleaned coverslip.
2. Place spores on the glass with very little distilled water and allow the drop to dry in air or an oven. Oven conditions are not given.
3. Take the prepared material to the microscopy facility for gold coating and imaging.

The source estimates approximately half an hour or less per sample and suggests bringing spores with both ornamented and non-ornamented walls.

## Slide mounting

| Material | Mounting preparation recorded in the source |
| --- | --- |
| AMF spores | One drop of PVLG plus spores |
| AMF spores, staining preparation | One drop of PVLG plus one drop of Melzer’s reagent plus spores |
| Fine roots | Drops of glycerol gelatin, warmed in a microwave, plus roots; heating conditions are not specified |
| Fungal spores | One drop of Trypan blue in lactophenol plus spores |
