## Sampling plan recorded in the source

AMF spores will be obtained from soil samples collected in the plant rhizosphere in each treatment and block of the planting areas in the state of Minas Gerais. Additional samples will be collected in reference areas (nearby preserved areas) within **[location missing]**. Samples will be transported to the laboratory.

Composite samples comprising 10 subsamples will be collected during the dry and rainy seasons in each area to assess the arbuscular mycorrhizal fungal community. AMF spores separated by wet sieving (Gerdemann & Nicolson, 1963) and purified will be collected and counted under a stereomicroscope. The number of AMF species will be transformed and statistically analyzed; means will be compared using Tukey’s test (P < 0.05) in Statistica.

Mycorrhization will be assessed (Pagano, 2007) only in treatments affected by cultivation of the species, considering the positive effects of their cultivation on sporulation and AMF species diversity, or their association with heavy metal accumulation.
