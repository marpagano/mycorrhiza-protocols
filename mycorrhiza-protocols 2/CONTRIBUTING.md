# Contributing

Open an issue to flag missing information, report a discrepancy or propose a change. Identify the page, protocol variant, current wording, proposed correction and supporting reference.

For a pull request, keep variants separate, identify the conditions under which the method was used, and document any change to values, units or step order. Fill a gap only with explicit supporting evidence. The custodians should record technical review and reviewer attribution.

Do not include third-party personal information or results that are not authorized for disclosure.
