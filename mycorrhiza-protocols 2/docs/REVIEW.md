# Review checklist

This checklist separates unresolved source content from editorial decisions needed for public release.

## Experimental content

- [ ] Confirm concentrations, percentage bases, times and temperatures in each root-staining variant.
- [ ] Resolve the instruction not to use phenol alongside the phenol-containing recipes.
- [ ] Specify the identity, purity and concentration of the reagent described as “ammonium” used for floor cleaning.
- [ ] Complete the English Trypan blue formulation, including glycerol volume and stain mass.
- [ ] Confirm glycerol units, the “liquefied” row and the initial stain name.
- [ ] Define the separate contexts for 50% and 70% alcohol preservation.
- [ ] Supply soil amount, sampling depth, locations and sieve mesh openings.
- [ ] Record rotor details and relative centrifugal force for spore extraction.
- [ ] Clarify sucrose concentration and the choice between 50% and 60%.
- [ ] Complete the medium, host system, antibiotic concentration and conditions for in vitro culture.
- [ ] Complete the container description, species combination and nutrient formulation for trap cultures.
- [ ] Recover and verify the Trouvelot formula and the attribution of colonization scales.
- [ ] Specify the statistical transformation and confirm its suitability for the study design.

## Publication preparation

- [ ] Provide authors, institution, contact and technical reviewer.
- [ ] Complete references and verify the origin of reproduced passages.
- [ ] Confirm reproduction rights and select a license.
- [ ] Select a repository name and description.
- [ ] Review and approve an initial dated or numbered release.
- [ ] Complete citation metadata and, if desired, archive a release with a DOI.
- [ ] Have a subject specialist check the English translation against the original document.

## Editorial treatment

Notes were separated into eight thematic Markdown pages. Variants remain separate. Missing values were not inferred. An informal personal attribution in the root-mounting notes was omitted. All repository prose, filenames and template field names are in English, apart from the original source filename, scientific names and proper names. The original uploaded document remains the source for comparison.
